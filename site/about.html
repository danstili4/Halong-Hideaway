From the peoplebehind your hostel.

Halong Hideaway is run by the team behind Central Backpackers and Buffalo Hostels. We built the trip we'd want to go on: a fair price, good food, good people and plenty to do.

The quiet side of the bay

Halong Bay is beautiful, and busy. We skip the crowds and head for Lan Ha Bay and Cat Ba Island: calmer water, empty beaches and the view from the top of Cat Ba National Park.

Your guide fills you in on life on the bay along the way.

The boat

The Hideaway is a 5-star cruise that anchors overnight in the bay, away from the busy routes.

  • 3Big shared jacuzzis. Most afternoons end in one.
  • 1Sundeck for doing nothing between activities.
  • 1Dance floor. You'll find it after the BBQ.

Eating

Every meal is included and cooked fresh on board, with a BBQ on deck each night. Vegetarian or no seafood? Just tell us when you book.

Sleeping

After the party, head down to a wood-panelled cabin with proper beds, fresh linen and a kettle for the morning coffee.

The boat anchors out in the bay, so you fall asleep on the water and wake up to the islands.
