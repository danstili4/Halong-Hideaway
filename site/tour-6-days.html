Two sides of northern Vietnam in six days: the full Hideaway cruise, then three days riding the Ha Giang Loop and staying with local families.

What's included

Days one to threeThe full Hideaway cruise

  1. Day 1

    Into Lan Ha Bay. Beach stop, boat jumping, banana tubing, beach volleyball, jacuzzis, sunset party and BBQ.

  2. Day 2

    Cycle through Viet Hai village, trek the Cat Ba jungle, fish massage lunch, then sunset party, round two.

  3. Day 3

    Morning kayaking if you're up for it, lunch on board, back in Hanoi around 4:30pm.

See the cruise hour by hour

Then a night bus north.

  1. 7:30pm

    Sleeper bus from Hanoi to Ha Giang.

  2. 3:00am

    Arrive in Ha Giang and sleep. That first bed is free.

Day fourHa Giang to Du Gia

  1. 120km

    Over Quan Ba pass, through the Dong Van Geopark and Tam Son.

  2. Night

    Homestay with a local ethnic minority family in Du Gia.

Day fiveDu Gia to Dong Van

  1. Ride

    Through Meo Vac, then a boat trip on the Nho Que river.

  2. Pass

    Over Ma Pi Leng pass to Dong Van.

  3. Night

    Hotel or homestay in Dong Van, with a hot pot dinner if you want it.

Day sixBack to Ha Giang City

  1. 150km

    Sa Phin palace and Yen Minh, then back over Tam Son and Quan Ba pass.

  2. 5:00pm

    Bus back to Hanoi, with a free hostel bed when you get in and breakfast the next morning (until 9:30am).

Before you go

Where we meet
7:30am at Hanoi Central Backpackers or Hanoi Buffalo Hostel, every day. Your big bag stays at the hostel for free.
What to pack
Passport, swimwear, sunscreen, insect repellent and a charged camera. Something warm from November to March.
Shoes
Proper trekking shoes for the Cat Ba jungle hike. Flip-flops won't cut it.
On the loop
Ride yourself or sit behind an experienced driver. Bike, fuel and guide are covered either way.
Food
Vegetarian and no-seafood meals are easy. For stricter diets, tell us when you book; you may need to bring some of your own food.
Confirm the day before
Stop by the travel desk the day before. Booking less than 24 hours out? Pay in full to hold your spot.
If plans change
Cancel 30 days to 48 hours out and you pay 25% of the fare. 48 to 24 hours out, 50%. Inside 24 hours, the full fare.
