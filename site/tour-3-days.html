The full Hideaway: two sunset parties, a day cycling and trekking Cat Ba Island, and kayaks on the last morning.

What's included

Day oneOut of the city, into the bay

  1. Morning

    Pickup from Central Backpackers or Buffalo Hostel in Hanoi. Leave your big bag with us for free, then it's bus and boat out past Cat Ba National Park.

  2. Arrival

    Lunch on board, then find your cabin.

  3. Afternoon

    A smaller boat takes you to a quiet beach. Jump off the boat, ride the banana tube, play volleyball, or sit in the sand with a cold beer. Then back on board for the jacuzzis.

Happy hour starts when the sun goes down.

  1. Sunset

    The boat heads to a quiet corner of the bay for the sunset party. Have your camera ready.

Then the party.

  1. 7:00

    BBQ dinner on deck.

  2. Later

    Dance floor, karaoke, beer pong, and a boat full of people you met this morning. Then you sleep on the water.

Day twoCat Ba on two wheels and two feet

  1. 7:00

    Breakfast in the boat's restaurant.

  2. 9:00

    Day boat to Cat Ba National Park, then cycle through Viet Hai village, past a tunnel and traditional houses.

  3. Trek

    Up through the jungle to a view over the village and the bay. Proper shoes for this one.

  4. Lunch

    At a local spot at the foot of the mountain, with a fish massage for tired feet.

Round two.

  1. Afternoon

    Back on board for a jacuzzi, a beer and the sunset.

  2. 7:00

    BBQ, then the party. By now everyone knows everyone.

Day threeQuiet water

  1. 8:30

    Guided kayaking through one of the calmest corners of the bay.

  2. Or

    Stay in the jacuzzi, on the sundeck or in bed. Your cabin's yours until lunch.

  3. 10:30

    Last lunch on board, then back to Hanoi.

  4. 4:30

    Back at the hostel, with a camera roll full of photos and a few new numbers.

Before you go

Where we meet
7:30am at Hanoi Central Backpackers or Hanoi Buffalo Hostel, every day. Your big bag stays at the hostel for free.
What to pack
Passport, swimwear, sunscreen, insect repellent and a charged camera. Something warm from November to March.
Shoes
Proper trekking shoes for the Cat Ba jungle hike. Flip-flops won't cut it.
Food
Vegetarian and no-seafood meals are easy. For stricter diets, tell us when you book; you may need to bring some of your own food.
Confirm the day before
Stop by the travel desk the day before. Booking less than 24 hours out? Pay in full to hold your spot.
If plans change
Cancel 30 days to 48 hours out and you pay 25% of the fare. 48 to 24 hours out, 50%. Inside 24 hours, the full fare.
